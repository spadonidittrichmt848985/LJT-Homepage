I am a first-year Ph.D. candidate at the HKUST NLP Group, Hong Kong University of Science and Technology, advised by Prof. Junxian He. My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning.

Research Interests
======
My research interests include:

* **LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning**
* **Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)**
* **LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability**
